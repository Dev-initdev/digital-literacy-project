# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist
### For College Students in India

---

## 🔐 Account & Password Safety

1. **Use strong, unique passwords** for every account. A strong password has at least 12 characters, mixing uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse the same password on two platforms.

2. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** on all important accounts — email, banking apps, social media. This adds a second verification step even if your password is stolen.

3. **Never share OTPs with anyone.** No legitimate bank, UPI app, government portal, or company will ever ask you for your One-Time Password over call, SMS, or chat.

---

## 💸 Financial / UPI Safety (Specific to Indian Students)

4. **Understand the difference between UPI SEND and COLLECT.**
   - A **collect request** requires YOU to send money to the requester.
   - You do NOT need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money — if someone asks you to enter your PIN to "receive" funds, it is a scam. Stop immediately.

5. **Never approve a UPI collect request from an unknown number.** Always call the person directly on a verified number before approving any payment request, no matter how small the amount seems.

---

## 🔗 Phishing & Link Safety

6. **Do not click links in unsolicited messages.** If you receive an SMS or WhatsApp message with a link claiming to be from NPCI, IRCTC, your bank, or a scholarship portal — do not click it. Go to the official website directly through your browser instead.

7. **Check the URL before entering any login or payment credentials.** Phishing websites often look identical to real ones but have slightly misspelled domain names (e.g., "sbi-secure.xyz" instead of "sbi.co.in").

---

## 🛡️ Personal Data & Privacy

8. **Never upload photos of your Aadhaar card, PAN card, student ID, or fee receipts** on social media or chat groups. These documents can be misused for identity theft and loan fraud.

9. **Review app permissions regularly.** On your smartphone, check which apps have access to your camera, microphone, contacts, and location. Revoke any permissions that are not clearly needed.

---

## 🆘 If You Are Victimised

- **Report immediately to the National Cyber Crime Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)
- **Call the 24x7 Cybercrime Helpline: 1930**
  - This is a Government of India helpline specifically for financial cybercrime. Reporting quickly increases the chance of a transaction freeze before funds are transferred.
- Inform your bank or UPI payment provider immediately to flag the transaction.
